# Job Tracker - phone app

This is the offline phone version of Job Tracker. It is just a web page: it contains **no data
and no accounts**. Everything you enter is stored on your own phone, in the browser's storage,
and is never sent to this website or anywhere else (the page's security policy forbids it).

## Install (iPhone)
1. Open this page in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open **Job Tracker** from the new icon on your Home Screen. Use only that icon from now on
   (Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate data).

After the first visit it works with no signal.

## Keeping your phone and computer in step
In the app tap **Sync / backup**. **Send my data** creates a small file you AirDrop or email to
your other device; **Receive** merges a file from the other device. Changes from both sides are
combined, and you see a summary before anything changes.
